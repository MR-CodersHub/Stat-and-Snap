The Studio Story

Founded By Former College Athletes & Sports Photographers

Stat & Snap was born out of frustration with boring, dated school sports photos. We set out to give student athletes the exact high-impact, magazine-worthy portraits seen in professional sports arenas.

Over the last decade, we have expanded from a single sports media van into a regional powerhouse serving over 120 school districts, athletic academies, and youth sports leagues.
