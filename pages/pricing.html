Pricing FAQ

Pricing Questions, Answered

Shipping, retakes, and school costs — no surprises.

Are there shipping charges for parent orders?
Direct-to-home shipping is a flat $5.99 per order. If the school selects bulk school delivery, shipping is 100% free!
Does the school pay anything upfront?
No. Parent-direct pay covers everything, and All-School partners earn a 10% revenue share back to the athletic department.
What if an athlete misses photo day?
A free retake day within 14 days is included. Absent athletes are composited into the team panorama at no charge.
Can parents mix bundles and add-ons?
Yes — banners, digitals, trader cards, and plaques can be added to any bundle in the same checkout.
