Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, protect, and retain school, athlete, and parent data — in plain language.

Effective Oct 01, 2026 6 min read FERPA-aware practices

Minimal Collection

Only what photo day needs: rosters, contact details, photos, and order info. Nothing more.

Locked-Down Galleries

Password-protected parent galleries. Student data encrypted and purged after production.

Your Rights First

Access, correct, or delete your data anytime — including full gallery removal on request.

01

Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to run photo days, deliver galleries, and sync yearbooks:

  • School contacts — AD/coach names, work emails, phone numbers, and shipping addresses from enquiry and booking forms.
  • Roster data — athlete names, grades, jersey numbers, and student IDs from official school CSV exports for QR matching and PSPA tagging.
  • Photographs — individual, team, and action images captured on photo day, including retake sessions.
  • Order & payment details — parent package selections and transaction records (payments are processed by PCI-compliant providers; we never store full card numbers).
  • Usage data — portal logins, gallery views, and device/cookie data that keeps proofing fast and secure.
02

How We Use Information

Every use ties directly to a service you asked for:

  • Running photo days — roster check-in, QR-matched capture, and coach coordination.
  • Delivering galleries — watermarked proofs, parent ordering, and direct-to-home or bulk shipping.
  • Yearbook sync — PSPA-formatted exports that drop into Jostens, Herff Jones, Balfour, and TreeRing.
  • Support & operations — scheduling, retake coordination, revenue-share payouts, and fraud prevention.

We do not sell personal data, and we never use student photos for advertising without explicit written consent.

03

Galleries & Data Sharing

Sharing is limited to the vendors required to fulfill your order:

  • Print labs & shippers — receive only the files and addresses needed for your prints.
  • Yearbook publishers — receive PSPA exports your school approves for release.
  • Payment & hosting providers — bound by contract to process data securely and for no other purpose.
  • Legal compliance — disclosed only when required by law or to protect student safety.
04

Cookies & Tracking

Our portals use a small set of cookies: strictly-necessary session and theme/direction preferences, plus aggregated analytics that help us speed up proofing. You can block non-essential cookies in your browser; core gallery and checkout features will continue to work.

05

Security & Retention

  • Encrypted galleries and restricted staff access — 100% background-checked crew.
  • Roster data is purged from active systems after production closes each season.
  • Ordered portraits are archived for reprints, then deleted on verified request.
  • $2M general liability coverage backs every on-campus assignment.
06

Your Rights & Choices

Parents, athletes (via guardians), and school staff may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data at any time — including full removal of a gallery. Schools may request FERPA-aligned handling of roster exports. To exercise any right, email privacy@statandsnap.com; we respond within 10 business days.

07

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or a data request? Reach our studio office at 742 Athletic Way, Suite 300, call (555) 832-7627, or email privacy@statandsnap.com. Material changes will be posted here with a revised effective date.
