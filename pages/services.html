Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Booking windows, absentees, and parent ordering — answered.

How early should a school book a photo day?
We recommend reserving photo day dates at least 4 to 6 weeks prior to the start of the athletic season to ensure optimal schedule availability.
What happens if an athlete is sick on photo day?
Every school booking includes a designated makeup/retake day. Absent athletes are photographed individually and seamlessly composited into the team photo.
How do parents view and order photos?
Parents receive a unique private access code via SMS/email. They can view watermarked proofs online, customize print packages, and ship directly to their home.
Does the school pay anything upfront?
No — there are zero upfront fees. Parents order direct, and All-School partners earn a 10% revenue share back to the athletic department.
