Terms of Service

The booking, payment, delivery, and licensing rules behind every Stat & Snap photo day.

Effective Oct 01, 2026 7 min read $0 upfront school fees

Book 4–6 Weeks Out

Fall and winter slots fill first. Early holds are free and movable once, guaranteed.

Free Retake Day

Every booking includes a makeup date within 14 days — absent athletes composited free.

You Own Your Prints

Parents get full personal-use rights on ordered portraits, plus PSPA files for yearbooks.

01

Services & Booking

Stat & Snap provides school sports photography: individual portraits, team photography, game coverage, yearbook sync, senior sessions, events, retakes, bulk contracts, and tournament coverage as listed on the Services page.

  • Reserve dates 4–6 weeks before the season; confirmed holds include gym access and a roster contact.
  • Schools provide accurate rosters, gym/field access with power-adjacent staging room, and a coach contact on shoot day.
  • Our background-checked crew arrives 60 minutes early and photographs roughly 60 athletes per hour.
02

Pricing & Payments

  • $0 upfront for schools — parent-direct pay covers standard bookings; published bundle prices apply at checkout.
  • Direct-to-home shipping is a flat $5.99 per order; bulk school delivery is free.
  • All-School contracts optionally include a 10% athletic-department revenue share, paid quarterly.
  • Overdue school invoices (30+ days) may pause future scheduling until resolved.
03

Retakes, Proofs & Delivery

  • Watermarked parent proofs arrive within 48 hours of shoot day (24-hour express on Varsity Premium).
  • One free retake/makeup date per season within 14 days; absentees are composited into team panoramas at no charge.
  • Print and banner timelines are quoted at order; lab delays are communicated with revised ETAs.
04

Cancellations & Rescheduling

Life happens — weather, gym conflicts, and emergencies included. Reschedule free of charge with 72+ hours notice; inside 72 hours we will still move the date once at no fee whenever crew allows. If we must cancel (equipment failure, crew emergency), you choose a priority re-shoot or a full refund of any school-paid amounts.

05

Photo License & Usage

  • Parents receive a perpetual personal-use license for ordered portraits (prints, gifts, social sharing).
  • Schools receive yearbook and editorial use of team/PSPA files plus gym display rights for banners.
  • Stat & Snap retains copyright and may showcase anonymized work in portfolios unless a school opts out in writing.
  • Student images are never sold to third parties or used in paid advertising without explicit consent.
06

Liability & Insurance

Every assignment carries $2M general liability coverage. To the extent permitted by law, our liability for any booking is limited to amounts paid for that booking, and neither party is liable for indirect or consequential losses. Force-majeure events (severe weather, facility closures, utility failures) extend timelines without penalty to either side.

07

Changes & Contact

We may update these terms as services evolve; continued bookings after posting constitute acceptance, and material changes are flagged in the school portal. Questions? Email info@statandsnap.com, call (555) 832-7627, or see our Privacy Policy for data handling.
